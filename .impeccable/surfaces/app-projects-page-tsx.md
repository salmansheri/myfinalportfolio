# Projects

## Scope and mode

Persuade, as a second page of the homepage's world (The Manifest; see the homepage brief). Route `/projects`, linked from the nav and from the Projects section on the homepage. Extends the established world; no new direction.

## Audience, job, action

The same four audiences, now checking whether the owner's past work matches what they need. They should see, per project, what it is, which layers the owner built, its stack and status, and reach the live site or repository; then email from the contact band.

## Proof and constraints

Projects come only from data/projects.ts; null fields render as AWAITING. Screenshots live in public/projects/. Entries are numbered 01, 02, …, with fields 01 Project, 02 What I built, 03 Status, 04 Tech stack and 05 Links, captioned "Fig. NN · project name". No pills, no card grid: one entry per project on the continuous form.

## Memorable moment

"What I built": ticked and open boxes for Data, API & services, Interface and Infrastructure make the end-to-end claim checkable per project.
